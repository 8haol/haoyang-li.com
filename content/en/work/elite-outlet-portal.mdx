## Context

End-of-line teamwear was sitting in the warehouse. Selling it through the main store would have undercut full-price ranges, and selling it by email meant manual invoices and stock mistakes. The company wanted a separate outlet that could run without anyone watching it.

## Constraints

- **No overselling.** Stock lives in the inventory system; the shop must never take money for a size that is gone.
- **Prices cannot be trusted from the browser.** Totals and coupons had to be recalculated on the server.
- **Finance needs a record.** Every paid order had to reach the finance team without anyone copying it across.

## My role

I designed and built the shop, the checkout functions and the stock sync, using an AI app builder for routine UI and writing the payment and inventory logic myself.

## Architecture

- **Storefront**: React and Vite on Base44; product data with colour and size variants, a basket, and a hero film.
- **Checkout**: a server function creates the Stripe Checkout session after re-validating every line's price and stock and applying coupons.
- **Stock**: the warehouse system pushes levels through an n8n webhook; the shop shows the lower of that and its own count, which is decremented when Stripe confirms payment.
- **Email**: order confirmation to the customer and an order summary to finance, both through Resend.

## Three decisions

**1. Two counts, take the minimum.** The warehouse feed can lag. Keeping a local count that drops on payment, and showing the lower of the two, closed the window where two people could buy the last item.

**2. Validate everything again on the server.** The browser only says what the customer wants; the checkout function decides what it costs and whether it is available.

**3. Stripe Checkout, not a custom payment form.** Hosted checkout meant no card data near the app, and wallets and receipts for free.

## Outcome

- Live at outlet.myclubgroup.com, selling end-of-line stock without manual invoicing.
- Stock is re-checked at checkout and decremented on payment, so a sold-out size cannot be bought.
- Finance receives every paid order automatically.

## What I'd do differently

Move the product catalogue out of code into the inventory system from the start. It began as a static list because it was quick, and every product change since has needed a deploy.

## Stack

React, Vite, Base44 with Deno functions, Stripe Checkout, Zoho inventory via n8n, Resend.
